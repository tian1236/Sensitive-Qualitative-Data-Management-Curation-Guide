1.1 Definition & Scope 1.1 概念与界定

Related Materials相关资料

Core Definition: Sensitive qualitative data 核心定义：敏感定性数据

Sensitive qualitative data refers to unstructured research materials — interviews, field notes, personal narratives — that, if disclosed, could cause identifiable harm to participants, including reputational, economic, social, or legal harm. 敏感定性数据指访谈、田野笔记、个人叙事等非结构化研究资料， 一旦泄露可能对受访者造成名誉、经济、社会或法律层面的可识别伤害。

Watch to Understand 📺 看个视频，更好理解

Unique Risks of Qualitative Data 定性数据的特殊风险

Contextual Re-identification 语境重识别

Unique experiences, locations and social ties can be combined to identify individuals, even without names. 即使没有姓名，独特经历、地点、社会关系也可组合定位到个人。

Narrative Harm 叙事伤害

Public disclosure of personal stories can cause stigma, social exclusion and damage to community relations. 个人叙事的公开可能带来污名化、社会排斥，破坏社群关系。

Unpredictable Secondary Use 不可控二次使用

Once public, future interpretation and dissemination of data cannot be predicted or controlled. 数据一旦公开，后续的解读与传播无法预判与控制。

Common Types of Sensitive Qualitative Data 常见敏感定性数据类型

  • Interview recordings & transcripts
  • Field observation notes & field diaries
  • Focus group discussion records
  • Visual qualitative data (photos, videos)
  • Archival personal narratives & letters

3 Common Misconceptions 三个常见认知误区

1. True or false: Removing names makes data anonymous.1. 判断：删掉名字，数据就是匿名的了。

Answer: False. Indirect identifiers and contextual details can still re-identify individuals.答案：错。间接标识符与语境细节仍可能重新识别个人。

2. True or false: If participants consent, data can be shared freely.2. 判断：受访者同意后，数据就可以随意共享。

Answer: False. Consent has boundaries, and researchers retain an ongoing duty to protect participants.答案：错。知情同意有边界，研究者仍负有持续保护受访者的责任。

3. True or false: Publicly available data has no sensitivity issues.3. 判断：公开获取的数据不存在敏感性问题。

Answer: False. Secondary use of public personal narratives can still create ethical risks and harm.答案：错。公开个人叙事的二次使用仍可能带来伦理风险与伤害。

Deep Dive: Academic Origins & Debates 深度拓展：学术溯源与争议讨论

Definitions across institutions: Repository and institutional guidance varies. Before deposit, compare the selected repository's current disclosure-risk, consent and access requirements rather than relying on a universal definition. 国际机构定义差异： 不同仓库与机构的指南并不完全相同。提交前应核对所选仓库当前的泄露风险、同意范围与访问要求，而不是依赖一个通用定义。

Contextual re-identification research: Direct identifiers may disclose identity alone, while combinations of occupation, place, rare events and other contextual details can create indirect disclosure risk in qualitative narratives. 语境重识别研究： 直接标识符本身可能揭示身份；职业、地点、罕见事件及其他语境细节的组合，也可能在定性叙事中形成间接泄露风险。

Gray areas: There is ongoing debate about observation in public spaces, data about public figures, and group-level harm where no individual is identifiable. 灰色地带： 公共场合观察、公众人物数据、无法识别个人但存在群体伤害的情形，目前仍存在持续争议。

Hazel’s Challenge Hazel 的挑战

Hazel wants to systematically assess how sensitive her interview data is, instead of relying on gut feeling which can easily miss risks. Hazel 想系统地评估自己的访谈数据敏不敏感， 凭感觉判断很容易漏掉风险点。

Method: Evaluate across four dimensions — identifiability, harm risk, content sensitivity and usage context. 方法：从可识别性、伤害风险、内容属性、使用场景四个维度逐项核对。

Usage Context使用场景 Who will access the data and how will it flow?数据会被哪些人接触、如何流转？ Intra-team sharing, restricted access, cross-institutional archiving, open release, unknown future secondary use研究者间共享、受限访问、跨机构归档、公开开放、未来未知二次使用 She plans to deposit transcripts into her university's open data platform after graduation — once archived, the flow and future use of data will move beyond her control, amplifying risk.她计划毕业后将访谈数据存入学校开放数据平台供同行查阅——一旦入库，数据流转范围和使用方式将脱离她的控制，风险随之放大。

Assessment approach: Consider identifiability, severity and likelihood of harm, participant vulnerability and the planned access environment together. One severe factor can justify escalation. 评估方法： 应综合可识别性、伤害的严重度与可能性、参与者脆弱性和计划访问环境；单个严重因素也可触发升级。

How readily could someone identify a participant from this dataset? 他人能多容易地通过这份数据定位到受访者？

If disclosed, what impact could this dataset have on participants or their community? 一旦泄露，这份数据可能给受访者或其社群带来怎样的影响？

Minimal or reversible inconvenience轻微且可逆的不便

Hazel’s Follow-up Question Hazel 的追问

Does all sensitive data need the highest level of protection? That seems inefficient and unnecessary. 是不是所有敏感数据都要最高级别防护？ 这样效率太低了，也没必要。

Match management intensity to risk level — neither relax requirements nor over-protect. 风险等级与管理强度相匹配 —— 既不放松要求，也不过度防护。

1.3 Three-Tier Risk Triage & Action Guide 1.3 三级风险分级与行动指南

Related Materials相关资料
1

Low Risk 低风险

Typical Profile 典型画像

De-identified, non-sensitive content, internal use only, no plausible re-identification route 已去标识化、不含敏感议题、仅内部使用、无合理识别路径

Typical Scenario 典型场景

Fully anonymised collective analysis summaries 完全脱敏的集体观点汇总分析

Core Actions 核心行动

Apply baseline security and institutional policies; routine file management and backup 执行基础安全措施与机构政策；常规文件管理与备份

2

Medium Risk 中风险

Typical Profile 典型画像

Indirect identifiers present, moderately sensitive topics, limited sharing, partial safeguards in place 含间接可识别信息、涉及一般敏感议题、小范围共享、有部分保护措施

Typical Scenario 典型场景

Pseudonymized transcripts; general health topic interviews 带伪名的访谈转录稿；普通健康话题访谈

Core Actions 核心行动

Standard de-identification; encrypted storage; access control; review residual risk before sharing 标准化去标识化；加密存储；访问权限控制；共享前复核剩余风险

3

High Risk 高风险

Typical Profile 典型画像

Direct or highly identifiable information, highly sensitive content, public/broad sharing planned, safeguards inadequate 含直接/高度可识别信息、高度敏感议题、计划公开或广泛共享、保护措施不足

Typical Scenario 典型场景

Verbatim interviews involving trauma, illegal conduct, rare identities or vulnerable groups 涉及创伤、违法行为、稀有身份或弱势群体的逐字访谈

Core Actions 核心行动

Formal ethics review; strict access control; document residual risk; consult IRB or legal counsel before sharing or archiving 正式伦理审查；严格权限控制；书面记录剩余风险；共享/归档前咨询 IRB 或法律顾问

⚠️ Important: This three-tier result is a learning tool — not a legal opinion, and not an ethics review. If your data contains direct identifiers, involves vulnerable populations (children, prisoners, trauma survivors), or could cause serious harm if disclosed, careful consideration is warrented — regardless of what this guide tells you.

⚠️ 重要说明：三级结果仅用于初步筛选，不代替专业判断或伦理委员会决定。只要涉及直接标识符、弱势群体或可信的严重伤害风险，即使综合判断为低风险，也应慎重考虑。
