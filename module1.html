Hazel’s Question Hazel 的疑问

Hazel looks at her interview transcripts and thinks: “I didn’t write down any names, so this can’t be sensitive data, right?” Hazel 看着自己的访谈转录稿，心想： “我又没写居民名字，这不算敏感数据吧？”

Key takeaway: Sensitivity depends on identifiability + harm risk, not just names. Context alone can re-identify individuals. 核心结论：敏感性取决于「可识别性 + 伤害风险」，而不只是有没有姓名。 仅凭语境信息就可能反向定位到个体。

1.1 Definition & Scope 1.1 概念与界定

Core Definition 核心定义

Sensitive qualitative data refers to unstructured research materials — interviews, field notes, personal narratives — that, if disclosed, could cause identifiable harm to participants, including reputational, economic, social, or legal harm. [source] 敏感定性数据指访谈、田野笔记、个人叙事等非结构化研究资料， 一旦泄露可能对受访者造成名誉、经济、社会或法律层面的可识别伤害。 [来源]

Unique Risks of Qualitative Data 定性数据的特殊风险

Contextual Re-identification 语境重识别

Unique experiences, locations and social ties can be combined to identify individuals, even without names. 即使没有姓名，独特经历、地点、社会关系也可组合定位到个人。

Narrative Harm 叙事伤害

Public disclosure of personal stories can cause stigma, social exclusion and damage to community relations. 个人叙事的公开可能带来污名化、社会排斥，破坏社群关系。

Unpredictable Secondary Use 不可控二次使用

Once public, future interpretation and dissemination of data cannot be predicted or controlled. 数据一旦公开，后续的解读与传播无法预判与控制。

Common Types of Sensitive Qualitative Data 常见敏感定性数据类型

  • Interview recordings & transcripts
  • Field observation notes & field diaries
  • Focus group discussion records
  • Visual qualitative data (photos, videos)
  • Archival personal narratives & letters

3 Common Misconceptions 三个常见认知误区

❌ Myth: Removing names makes data anonymous ❌ 误区：删掉名字数据就是匿名的了
✅ Fact: Indirect identifiers + contextual details can still re-identify individuals with high accuracy. ✅ 真相：间接标识符 + 语境细节依然可以高准确率地识别到个人。
❌ Myth: If participants consent, data can be shared freely ❌ 误区：受访者同意了就可以随便共享数据
✅ Fact: Consent has clear boundaries. Researchers have an ongoing duty to protect participants beyond consent. ✅ 真相：知情同意有明确边界，研究者负有超越同意的持续保护责任。
❌ Myth: Publicly available data has no sensitivity issues ❌ 误区：公开获取的数据就没有敏感问题
✅ Fact: Secondary use of public personal narratives still carries ethical risks and potential harm. ✅ 真相：公开个人叙事的二次使用依然存在伦理风险与潜在伤害。
📚 Deep Dive: Academic Origins & Debates 📚 深度拓展：学术溯源与争议讨论

Definitions across institutions: Repository and institutional guidance varies. Before deposit, compare the selected repository's current disclosure-risk, consent and access requirements rather than relying on a universal definition. [source] 国际机构定义差异： 不同仓库与机构的指南并不完全相同。提交前应核对所选仓库当前的泄露风险、同意范围与访问要求，而不是依赖一个通用定义。 [来源]

Contextual re-identification research: Direct identifiers may disclose identity alone, while combinations of occupation, place, rare events and other contextual details can create indirect disclosure risk in qualitative narratives. [source] 语境重识别研究： 直接标识符本身可能揭示身份；职业、地点、罕见事件及其他语境细节的组合，也可能在定性叙事中形成间接泄露风险。 [来源]

Gray areas: There is ongoing debate about observation in public spaces, data about public figures, and group-level harm where no individual is identifiable. 灰色地带： 公共场合观察、公众人物数据、无法识别个人但存在群体伤害的情形，目前仍存在持续争议。

Hazel’s Challenge Hazel 的挑战

Hazel wants to systematically assess how sensitive her interview data is, instead of relying on gut feeling which can easily miss risks. Hazel 想系统地评估自己的访谈数据敏不敏感， 凭感觉判断很容易漏掉风险点。

Method: Evaluate across four dimensions — identifiability, harm risk, content sensitivity and usage context. 方法：从可识别性、伤害风险、内容属性、使用场景四个维度逐项核对。

1.2 Four Dimensions of Sensitivity Assessment 1.2 四维度判定标准

Dimension 判定维度 Core Question 核心问题 Sensitive Indicators 敏感特征 Hazel’s Case Hazel 的案例
Identifiability Can individuals be directly or indirectly identified? 能否直接/间接定位到具体个体？ Names, contact info, unique traits, specific locations 姓名、联系方式、独特特征、具体地点 Specific foraging areas & unique life events → indirect identification possible 含具体觅食区域与独特经历 → 可间接识别
Harm Risk How much harm would disclosure cause? 泄露后对受访对象有多大伤害？ Legal risk, stigma, health privacy, employment risk 法律风险、污名化、健康隐私、就业风险 Habitat disclosure would disrupt lives; private experiences could harm community relations 栖息地泄露会打扰生活；隐秘经历可能破坏社群关系
Content Sensitivity Is the content legally/ethically sensitive? 内容本身是否属于敏感范畴？ Health, religion, sexuality, politics, illegal activities 健康、宗教、性取向、政治观点、违法经历 Rare group identities, private survival struggles, internal community matters 涉及稀有族群身份、私人生存困境、社群内部事务
Usage Context How widely will the data be accessed? 数据会被多少人接触、是否公开？ Cross-institutional sharing, public archiving, open publication 跨机构共享、公开归档、公开发布 Planned deposit in university repository for future researcher access 计划存入学校数据仓库，供后续研究者查阅

Assessment approach: Do not use a fixed count as a legal threshold. Consider identifiability, severity and likelihood of harm, participant vulnerability and the planned access environment together. One severe factor can justify escalation. 评估方法： 不要把固定项数当作法律阈值。应综合可识别性、伤害的严重度与可能性、参与者脆弱性和计划访问环境；单个严重因素也可触发升级。

Interactive Self-Assessment 交互式快速自测

Q1: Can your data be used to identify specific individuals (directly or indirectly)? Q1：你的数据能否（直接或间接）定位到具体个人？

Yes, clearly 是，很明显
No, basically not 否，基本不能

Q2: Would data disclosure cause tangible harm to participants? Q2：数据泄露是否会对受访者造成实质性伤害？

Yes, significant harm 是，会有明显伤害
No, minimal harm 否，几乎没有伤害

Q3: Does the content involve sensitive topics (health, identity, illegal acts, etc.)? Q3：内容是否涉及敏感议题（健康、身份、违法行为等）？

Yes, highly sensitive 是，高度敏感
No, general topics 否，属于普通议题

Q4: Do you plan public or broad external access rather than controlled access? Q4：你是否计划公开或广泛外部访问，而不是受控访问？

Yes, public or broad external access 是，公开或广泛外部访问
No, internal or controlled access 否，仅内部或受控访问

📚 Deep Dive: Theoretical Basis & Boundaries 📚 深度拓展：理论依据与边界界定

Legal definition of identifiability: PDPO and GDPR differ slightly in their interpretation of "personal data", but both adopt a broad standard including indirect identifiability. 可识别性的法律界定： PDPO 与 GDPR 对「个人数据」的解释略有差异，但均采用包含间接识别的宽泛标准。

Five dimensions of harm: Physical harm, reputational harm, economic harm, social harm, legal harm. Assessment should consider both individual and group-level harm. [guide note] 伤害的五个维度： 身体伤害、名誉伤害、经济伤害、社会伤害、法律伤害。 评估时需同时考虑个体层面与群体层面的伤害。 [指南说明]

Hazel’s Follow-up Question Hazel 的追问

Does all sensitive data need the highest level of protection? That seems inefficient and unnecessary. 是不是所有敏感数据都要最高级别防护？ 这样效率太低了，也没必要。

Principle: Match management intensity to risk level — neither relax requirements nor over-protect. 原则：风险等级与管理强度相匹配 —— 既不放松要求，也不过度防护。

1.3 Three-Tier Risk Classification 1.3 三级风险分级体系

Low 低

Low Risk 低风险

Criteria 判定标准

No identifiable information; non-sensitive content; internal team use only 无可识别信息；内容不涉及敏感议题；仅内部团队使用

Typical Scenario 典型场景

Fully anonymized collective analysis summaries 完全脱敏的集体观点汇总分析

Core Requirements 核心要求

Basic file management; routine backup 基础文件管理；常规备份

Medium 中

Medium Risk 中风险

Criteria 判定标准

Indirect identifiers present; moderately sensitive content; limited sharing 含间接可识别信息；内容涉及一般敏感议题；小范围共享

Typical Scenario 典型场景

Pseudonymized transcripts; general health topic interviews 带伪名的访谈转录稿；普通健康话题访谈

Core Requirements 核心要求

Standard de-identification; encrypted storage; access control 标准化去标识化；加密存储；访问权限控制

High 高

High Risk 高风险

Criteria 判定标准

Direct/highly identifiable information; highly sensitive content; public sharing planned 含直接/高度可识别信息；高度敏感议题；计划公开共享

Typical Scenario 典型场景

Verbatim interviews involving trauma, illegal conduct, rare identities or vulnerable groups 涉及创伤、违法行为、稀有身份或弱势群体的逐字访谈

Core Requirements 核心要求

Formal ethics review; strict access control; documented residual-risk review; controlled sharing only 正式伦理审查；严格权限控制；记录剩余风险评估；仅限受控共享

Important: This three-tier result is an educational triage, not a legal or IRB determination. Direct identifiers, vulnerable populations, or a credible risk of serious harm should trigger specialist review even when the total score is low. Source note

重要说明：三级结果仅用于教育性初筛，不代替法律判断或伦理委员会决定。只要涉及直接标识符、弱势群体或可信的严重伤害风险，即使总分较低也应升级为专业审查。来源说明
