SQDMC Guide (Sensitive Qualitative Data Management & Curation Guide) is an interactive learning guide for humanities and social sciences researchers. It addresses a deceptively simple but profoundly difficult question: when researchers collect unquantifiable "human stories", how can they protect participants' privacy while ensuring that the data remains reusable and academically valuable?
The guide is organized into five core learning modules, leading readers step by step from foundational concepts to data management and advanced curation. And it designed a cartoon character. By following her research journey, learners are not passively receiving knowledge but actively experiencing the entire process of data handling and curation. The guide also includes a glossary and reference list to support its claims, embeds interactive features such as risk self‑assessment tools and practice case studies, and has a curated collection of resources to meet readers' additional learning needs.
