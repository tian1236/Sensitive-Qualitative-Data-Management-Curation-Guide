1. De-identification Worksheet 1. 去标识化工单

Systematic tracking of redaction decisions for audit and quality control 系统记录脱敏决策，用于审计与质量控制

Use this worksheet to document every de-identification decision during processing. It serves as an audit trail and ensures consistency across multiple reviewers. 使用本工作表记录处理过程中每一项去标识化决策，作为审计追踪， 并确保多名审核人员之间的标准一致。

=== DATA USE AGREEMENT (DUA) === Parties: - Data Provider: [Institution / Research Team Name] - Data Recipient: [Name], [Institution], [Position] - Project Title: [Title of research project using the data] 1. GRANT OF ACCESS The Data Provider grants the Data Recipient limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable access to the dataset described in Appendix A, solely for the purpose of the research project stated above. 2. PERMITTED USES - Non-commercial academic research only - Secondary analysis and replication studies - Publication of aggregated results and quotations (provided no individual is identifiable) 3. PROHIBITED USES - Any attempt to identify or re-identify individual participants - Commercial use, marketing or advertising - Redistribution, sharing or transfer of the data to any third party - Use of the data for decisions affecting individual persons - Modification or derivative redistribution of the dataset 4. SECURITY OBLIGATIONS The Recipient agrees to: - Store the data on secure, password-protected devices - Apply full-disk encryption on all devices containing the data - Not store the data on public cloud services or shared drives without explicit written approval - Limit access to only named researchers on the approved project - Report any suspected data breach to the Provider immediately 5. CONFIDENTIALITY The Recipient shall maintain strict confidentiality of all data and take all reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access. No information that could identify participants shall be disclosed in any publication or presentation. 6. CITATION & ATTRIBUTION All publications, presentations and outputs using this dataset must include the formal dataset citation as specified in Appendix B. 7. TERM & TERMINATION - Agreement effective from [start date] to [end date] - Provider may terminate access immediately if Recipient breaches any terms of this agreement - Upon termination or project completion, Recipient must securely destroy all copies of the data and provide written confirmation 8. DISCLAIMER & LIMITATION OF LIABILITY The dataset is provided "as is". The Provider makes no warranties regarding accuracy, completeness or fitness for a particular purpose. In no event shall the Provider be liable for any damages arising from use of the data. 9. GOVERNING LAW This agreement shall be governed by the laws of [jurisdiction]. Signatures: Data Provider: _________________________ Date: __________ Data Recipient: _______________________ Date: __________ Appendix A: Dataset Description [list of files, de-identification tier, access mode] Appendix B: Preferred Citation [standard dataset citation format] === 数据使用协议（DUA）=== 协议双方： - 数据提供方：[机构 / 研究团队名称] - 数据接收方：[姓名]，[所属机构]，[职位] - 项目名称：[使用数据的研究项目标题] 1. 访问授权 数据提供方授予数据接收方有限的、非排他的、不可转让的数据集访问权限， 仅可用于上述研究项目之目的。数据集描述见附录 A。 2. 允许用途 - 仅限非商业学术研究 - 二次分析与复制研究 - 发布汇总结果与引语（前提是无法识别任何个人身份） 3. 禁止用途 - 任何识别或重新识别参与者个人身份的尝试 - 商业使用、营销或广告 - 向任何第三方再分发、共享或转让数据 - 使用数据做出影响个人的决策 - 修改数据集或衍生再分发 4. 安全义务 接收方承诺： - 将数据存储于安全的、有密码保护的设备 - 所有含数据的设备开启全盘加密 - 未经书面批准，不得存储于公共云服务或共享磁盘 - 仅限获批项目内的指定研究人员访问 - 一旦怀疑数据泄露，立即通知提供方 5. 保密义务 接收方应对所有数据严格保密，采取一切合理措施防止未授权访问。 任何可能识别参与者的信息均不得在出版物或报告中披露。 6. 引用与署名 所有使用本数据集的出版物、报告与成果，均须按附录 B 规定的格式 正式引用本数据集。 7. 期限与终止 - 协议有效期：[起始日期] 至 [结束日期] - 若接收方违反本协议任何条款，提供方可立即终止访问权限 - 协议终止或项目结束时，接收方须安全销毁所有数据副本， 并提供书面确认 8. 免责与责任限制 数据集按「现状」提供。提供方不对数据的准确性、完整性 或特定用途适用性做任何保证。任何情况下，提供方均不对 因使用数据而产生的损害承担责任。 9. 适用法律 本协议受 [司法辖区] 法律管辖。 签字： 数据提供方：_________________________ 日期：__________ 数据接收方：_______________________ 日期：__________ 附录 A：数据集说明 [文件清单、去标识化级别、访问模式] 附录 B：推荐引用格式 [标准数据集引用格式]

5. FAQ Quick Reference 5. 常见问题速查

Answers to the most common practical questions 最常见实操问题的简明解答

Q1: Can I publish interview transcripts openly? Q1：访谈转录稿可以完全开放发布吗？

In most cases, fully open publication is not recommended for verbatim qualitative transcripts. Even with names removed, unique combinations of details (occupation, location, life events) can often re-identify participants. For most sensitive qualitative data, registered or controlled access is the more ethically appropriate choice. Strictly de-identified excerpts may be published as supplementary materials.

大多数情况下，不建议逐字定性转录稿完全公开发布。 即使移除了姓名，独特的细节组合（职业、地点、人生事件） 往往仍能重新识别参与者。对于多数敏感定性数据， 注册访问或受控访问是伦理上更合适的选择。 经过严格脱敏的片段可作为补充材料发布。

Q2: What if participants did not consent to data sharing? Q2：如果参与者没有同意数据共享怎么办？

Do not assume that de-identification alone authorises sharing. Check the participant information and consent process, ethics approval, applicable legal basis and any research exceptions. If no valid authority can be established, do not share participant-level data. Options include: (1) archive only aggregated findings, not individual-level data; (2) contact participants to request additional consent for sharing; (3) retain data securely within your institution only, without external archiving.

不要假定去标识化本身就能授权共享。应核查参与者说明与同意过程、伦理审批、适用法律依据及研究例外；如果无法确立有效权限，就不要共享参与者层面的数据。可选方案包括： （1）仅归档汇总层面的研究发现，不归档个体数据； （2）联系参与者，追加征求共享同意； （3）数据仅在机构内安全留存，不对外归档。

Q3: How long should I retain raw research data? Q3：原始研究数据应该留存多久？

Retention periods vary by law, institution, funder, discipline and approved protocol. There is no universal 3–7 year rule. Sensitive data should not be kept longer than necessary. Check your funder policy, institutional rules and ethics approval conditions. Curated archived datasets may be retained long-term under controlled access.

留存期限因法律、机构、资助方、学科与获批方案而异， 不存在通用的“发表后3–7年”规则。 敏感数据不应超过必要期限留存。 请核查资助方政策、机构规定与伦理审批条件。 策展后的归档数据集可在受控访问下长期留存。

Q4: Can AI / NLP tools fully replace manual de-identification? Q4：AI / NLP 自动工具能完全替代人工去标识化吗？

No. Automated tools can efficiently detect and remove standard named entities (names, organizations, locations, dates), but they cannot reliably identify contextual re-identification risks — unique combinations of life events, occupation details, community-specific references that only a human reviewer familiar with the research context can spot.

Automated tools are useful as a first-pass screening step, but documented human review is essential. Whether two independent reviewers are required should follow the project's risk assessment, ethics approval, repository rules and available resources.

不能。自动工具可以高效检测并移除标准命名实体（姓名、机构、地点、日期）， 但无法可靠识别语境重识别风险——独特的人生事件组合、职业细节、 社群专属指代，这些只有熟悉研究语境的人工审核者才能发现。

自动工具适合作为初筛步骤，但敏感定性数据 必须开展有记录的人工复核；是否需要两名独立审核人，应依据项目风险评估、伦理审批、仓库规则与可用资源决定。

Q5: Which license should I choose for qualitative data? Q5：定性数据集应该选择什么使用协议？

For most sensitive qualitative datasets shared under controlled access, a custom Data Use Agreement (DUA) is more appropriate than a standard open license, as it can enforce confidentiality, purpose limitation and non-identification clauses.

If you share fully de-identified, aggregated data openly, common options include:

  • CC BY 4.0: A widely used attribution-based option
  • CC BY-NC 4.0: Non-commercial use only

A license cannot cure an ethics, consent, privacy or disclosure-risk problem. Before applying any open license or CC0, confirm that open release is authorised and that the released material meets the applicable anonymisation and repository criteria.

对于大多数受控访问下共享的敏感定性数据集，自定义的 数据使用协议（DUA）比标准开放协议更合适， 因为它可以强制执行保密、用途限定、禁止识别等条款。

如果公开共享完全脱敏的汇总数据，常用选项包括：

  • CC BY 4.0：要求署名，复用限制最宽松
  • CC BY-NC 4.0：仅限非商业使用

许可协议不能消除伦理、同意、隐私或泄露风险问题。在采用任何开放许可或 CC0 前， 应先确认公开发布已有授权，并且拟发布材料满足适用的匿名化标准和仓储要求。
