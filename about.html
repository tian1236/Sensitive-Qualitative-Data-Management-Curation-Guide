About this guide关于本指南

Purpose目的

An educational workflow for planning the management, ethical review, curation and controlled reuse of sensitive qualitative research data.

为敏感定性研究数据的管理、伦理审查、策展与受控复用提供教育性工作流程。

Recommended sequence推荐顺序

Risk triage → legal and ethics determination → lifecycle controls → archive-or-destroy decision → evidence and templates.

风险初筛 → 法规与伦理认定 → 全生命周期控制 → 归档或销毁决策 → 证据与模板。

Limitations局限

The guide does not determine jurisdiction, legal basis, anonymisation status or IRB outcome. Local law, institutional policy, sponsor conditions and repository requirements prevail.

本指南不能自动决定管辖范围、法律依据、匿名化状态或伦理审查结果；当地法律、机构政策、资助条件与仓库要求优先。
