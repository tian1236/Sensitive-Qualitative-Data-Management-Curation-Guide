Hazel’s Confusion Hazel 的困惑

Isn’t archiving just uploading files to a repository? Why do I need to learn “data curation” as a separate skill? 归档不就是把文件传到仓库里吗？ 为什么还要专门学「数据策展」？

Key difference: Basic management keeps data safe; curation ensures data remains usable, understandable and valuable long-term. 核心区别：基础管理保证数据「不出错」， 策展保证数据长期「有用、可读、有价值」。

4.1 What is Data Curation? 4.1 什么是数据策展

Data curation is the active, ongoing management of data throughout its entire lifecycle, to ensure its long-term availability, authenticity, understandability and reusability while maintaining ethical and privacy compliance. 数据策展是在数据整个生命周期中进行的主动、持续管理， 确保其长期可用性、真实性、可理解性与可复用性， 同时保持伦理与隐私合规。

Three Core Challenges for Sensitive Qualitative Curation 敏感定性数据策展的三大核心挑战

Fidelity-Safety Tradeoff 保真-安全的权衡

More de-identification reduces risk but also erodes the research value of rich narrative data. 脱敏程度越高，风险越低，但丰富叙事数据的研究价值也随之衰减。

Contextual Re-identification 语境重识别风险

Unique combinations of details in narratives can re-identify individuals even without explicit names. 叙事中独特的细节组合，即使没有姓名也可能重新识别出个人。

Standardization Difficulty 标准化难度高

Unstructured qualitative data is harder to standardize than structured quantitative datasets. 非结构化定性数据相比结构化定量数据，更难实现标准化归档。

Tripartite Balance Model 三角平衡模型

High-quality curation balances three core values: Ethical Safety, Semantic Fidelity and Reusability. Different archiving scenarios prioritize different dimensions. 高质量的策展需要在三个核心价值之间取得平衡： 伦理安全、语义保真度与可复用性。 不同的归档场景对应不同的优先级。

Deep Dive: Academic Origins & Frontiers 深度拓展：学术溯源与前沿方向

Institutional development: QDR (Qualitative Data Repository) at Syracuse University is the world’s leading dedicated qualitative data curation institution, establishing many of the field’s standards and best practices. 机构发展： 锡拉丘兹大学的QDR（定性数据仓库）是全球领先的专业定性数据策展机构， 奠定了该领域的诸多标准与最佳实践。

Frontier directions: Contextual anonymization, dynamic de-identification, community-governed data, and Indigenous data sovereignty are active areas of research. 前沿方向： 语境匿名化、动态去标识化、社群治理数据、原住民数据主权等都是当前活跃的研究方向。

Interdisciplinarity: Data curation sits at the intersection of library science, information studies, computer science and research ethics. 跨学科属性： 数据策展处于图书馆学、情报学、计算机科学与研究伦理的交叉领域。

Hazel’s Dilemma Hazel 的难题

If I redact too much, the data loses its research value. If I redact too little, participants could be identified. How do I find the right balance? 删多了数据就没研究价值了，删少了又可能识别出受访者。 怎么找到合适的平衡点？

Answer: There is no fully anonymous dataset that fully preserves research value. De-identification is a privacy–utility trade-off. The goal is a defensible balance for your research purpose, data and sharing context. 回答：不存在“既完全匿名又完整保留研究价值”的数据集：去标识化是隐私与效用之间的权衡。目标是为你的研究目的、数据和共享情境找到可辩护的平衡。

4.2 De-identification Methodology 4.2 去标识化方法论

Practical Principles实操原则

01

Start from intended use从用途倒推

Protect the analytical capabilities needed after sharing, then configure privacy controls. Value means answering intended questions—not retaining the most details.先保住共享后要回答研究问题所需的分析能力，再配置隐私保护。价值不等于保留最多细节。

02

Find quasi-identifiers识别准标识符

Names are only the start. Postal codes, dates, places, rare traits and relationship clues can combine to identify someone—especially to insiders.姓名只是开始。邮编、日期、地点、罕见特征和关系线索可被组合识别，尤其对熟悉社区的“内人”。

03

Transform step by step分步变换

Generalise first, then suppress locally. Focus changes on secondary or high-risk variables rather than making one large cut.先泛化，再局部抑制。把变换集中于次要或高风险变量，而非一次大删减。

Judgment Framework判断框架

Research utility研究效用

Can key analyses still run?关键分析还能不能做？

Re-identification risk重识别风险

Are records still unique or linkable?记录是否仍过于独特或可链接？

Contextual risk情境风险

Who has access and what can they link?谁会接触？可关联哪些外部资料？

Sharing mode共享方式

Open or restricted access?公开还是受限访问？

The right point is where protection is sufficient but utility has not begun to collapse—not where risk is absolutely minimal.合适的平衡点是“够安全”且研究效用尚未明显塌陷的位置，而不是把风险降到绝对最低。

How to Verify验证方法

Assess your approach from two perspectives.验证应从两个方面考虑。

1

Attack Assessment攻击评估

Assess linkage attacks, attribute disclosure and identity disclosure.评估链接攻击、属性披露与身份披露风险。

Tools you can use: 可使用工具协助：ARX ↗ · sdcMicro ↗

2

Analytical Assessment分析评估

Re-run the intended analysis on de-identified data and compare it with the original results. If conclusions change materially, revisit the transformations.在去标识化数据上重新运行预定分析，并与原始结果比较；若分析结论发生实质变化，应重新审视变换方案。

Guide 5-Step Review Workflow 本指南五步复核流程

An operational review sequence synthesised for this guide; adapt it to the selected repository and approved protocol. 本指南归纳的操作性复核顺序；应结合所选仓库与获批方案进行调整。

1

Direct identifier removal & verification 直接标识符移除与核验

Systematically scan and remove all explicit identifiers; double-check for completeness. 系统扫描并移除所有显式标识符，双人复核确保无遗漏。

2

Pseudonym system design 伪名体系设计

Create consistent IDs for all persons, places and organizations; store key separately and encrypted. 为所有人物、地点、机构建立统一代号；密钥单独加密存储。

3

Context generalization 上下文信息泛化

Replace specific locations with regions, exact dates with time periods, precise roles with categories. 具体地点→区域；精确日期→时间段；具体职位→类别。

4

Unique trait screening & removal 独特特征筛查与移除

Identify rare events, unusual biographies and other details that could uniquely identify someone. 识别罕见事件、特殊履历等可能唯一锁定个人的细节并做处理。

5

Dual-person cross-check & QA 双人交叉复核与质量保证

Two independent reviewers verify de-identification quality; document the review process. 两名独立审核人员核验脱敏质量，留存审核记录。

Source and limitation note: UK Data Service guidance supports planned, risk-based qualitative anonymisation. The tier labels and five steps here are this guide's operational synthesis, not a named external standard.来源与局限说明：英国数据服务指南支持预先规划、按风险实施定性资料匿名化；本页的三级名称与五步流程是本指南的操作性归纳，并非具名外部标准。

Fidelity Judgment Principles 保真度判断原则

  • Must keep: ✅ 必须保留： Core arguments, narrative logic, key quotes directly related to the research question. 与研究问题直接相关的核心论点、叙事逻辑、关键引语。
  • May generalize: ⚠️ 可以泛化： Contextual details that do not affect research conclusions (specific dates, exact locations). 不影响研究结论的语境细节（具体日期、精确地点等）。
  • Must remove: ❌ 必须移除： Any information that can directly or highly probably identify individuals. 所有可直接或高概率识别到个人的信息。
Deep Dive: Theoretical Framework & Limitations 深度拓展：理论框架与局限性

The impossibility of perfect anonymity: No de-identification method can guarantee 100% non-identifiability for rich qualitative data. Describe reasonably foreseeable residual risks in the consent process and repository documentation as required by the approved protocol. 完美匿名的不可能性： 对于丰富的定性数据，没有任何去标识化方法能保证100%不可识别。 按获批方案要求，在知情同意过程与仓库文档中说明合理可预见的残余风险。

Automated tools vs. manual review: NLP tools can efficiently remove named entities, but cannot reliably detect contextual re-identification risks. Documented human review remains important; its design should be proportionate to risk. 自动工具与人工复核： NLP工具可高效移除命名实体，但无法可靠识别语境重识别风险；仍应进行有记录且与风险相称的人工复核。

Hazel’s Question Hazel 的疑问

Why do I need to write detailed metadata? Isn’t it enough to just upload the data files with a short description? 为什么一定要写详细的元数据？ 上传文件的时候加一段简短说明不够吗？

Principle: Metadata is the instruction manual for data. Without standardized metadata, future researchers cannot understand or reuse the data. 原则：元数据就是数据的说明书。没有标准化元数据，未来的研究者无法理解和复用数据。

4.3 Metadata Standards 4.3 元数据标准

Four Core Metadata Categories 四类核心元数据

1. Descriptive Metadata (for discovery) 1. 描述性元数据（用于发现）

Help others find and understand the dataset at a glance. 帮助他人快速发现并从整体上理解数据集。

Required fields: Title, researcher(s), abstract, keywords, data type, collection date, geographic scope 必备字段：标题、研究者、摘要、关键词、数据类型、采集时间、地理范围

2. Administrative Metadata (for preservation) 2. 管理性元数据（用于保存）

Ensure long-term preservability and technical authenticity of the dataset. 保障数据集的长期可保存性与技术真实性。

Required fields: File format, file size, checksum, creation date, software used, version history, access rights 必备字段：文件格式、文件大小、校验值、创建时间、使用软件、版本历史、访问权限

3. Structural Metadata (for comprehension) 3. 结构性元数据（用于理解）

Explain how files are organized, coded and related to each other. 说明文件之间的组织方式、编码逻辑与相互关系。

Required fields: File structure, coding scheme, variable definitions, data collection methodology, sampling strategy 必备字段：文件结构、编码方案、变量定义、数据采集方法、抽样策略

4. Ethical Metadata (for compliance) 4. 伦理性元数据（用于合规）

Document ethical approval, consent scope and usage constraints. 记录伦理审批情况、同意范围与使用限制。

Required fields: IRB approval number, consent type, de-identification tier, access mode, permitted uses, citation requirements 必备字段：IRB审批号、同意类型、去标识化级别、访问模式、允许用途、引用要求

Deep Dive: Standard Specifications & Community Norms 深度拓展：标准规范与社群惯例

Mainstream standards: DDI is a maintained suite for documenting social, behavioural, economic and health research data; select the DDI product and repository schema that fit the dataset. Dublin Core is a general-purpose option. 主流标准： DDI是一套用于记录社会、行为、经济与健康研究数据的持续维护规范；应选择适合数据集的DDI产品与仓库模式。Dublin Core可作为通用方案。

Qualitative-specific standards: Qualitative repositories may require repository-specific fields for methods, context, consent and access restrictions; always validate the current deposit requirements of the selected repository. 定性专属标准： 定性数据仓库可能要求方法、语境、同意范围及访问限制等专门字段；应始终核对所选仓库当前的提交要求。

FAIR principles: FAIR means Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable; "Accessible" can include authentication and authorisation, so FAIR does not require sensitive data to be open. FAIR 原则： FAIR指可发现、可访问、可互操作、可复用；“可访问”可以包含认证与授权，因此FAIR不要求敏感数据必须开放。

Sources: 来源：DDI Alliance · FAIR Principles · DataCite Metadata Schema

Hazel’s Question Hazel 的疑问

Where should I deposit my qualitative data? A general repository, or one specifically for qualitative data? 定性数据应该存在哪里？通用仓库好，还是专门的定性数据仓库好？

Principle: Choose repository based on data sensitivity, disciplinary norms and access mode. Sensitive qualitative data is best suited for specialized qualitative data repositories. 原则：根据数据敏感度、学科惯例、访问模式选择仓库。敏感定性数据优先选择专业定性数据仓库。

6-Step Standard Submission Workflow 六步标准提交流程

1

Pre-submission compliance check 提交前合规检查

Verify IRB approval, consent scope, and that de-identification meets repository requirements. 核验伦理审批、同意范围、去标识化程度是否符合仓库要求。

2

Data file organization & formatting 数据文件整理与格式化

Organize files into logical folders; convert to open, sustainable formats; standardize naming conventions. 按逻辑整理文件夹结构，转换为开放可持续格式，统一命名规范。

3

Prepare complete metadata documentation 准备完整元数据文档

Complete all required metadata fields according to repository specifications. 按仓库规范填写所有必填元数据字段。

4

Select access mode & usage license 选择访问模式与使用协议

Choose open / registered / controlled access; select appropriate data usage license. 选择开放/注册/受控访问模式，选用合适的数据使用许可协议。

5

Upload files & submit for review 上传文件并提交审核

Upload all files via secure channel; submit for repository curatorial review. 通过安全通道上传所有文件，提交仓库策展审核。

6

Obtain DOI & complete citation documentation 获取 DOI 并完善引用信息

After publication, obtain persistent DOI; add citation information to papers and project outputs. 发布后获取永久 DOI，在论文与项目成果中补充引用信息。

Persistent Identifier (DOI) & Citation 永久标识符与引用规范

  • All formally archived datasets should obtain a DOI (Digital Object Identifier) for persistent, citable access. 所有正式归档的数据集都应获取 DOI（数字对象标识符），实现永久可访问、可引用。
  • When publishing papers, cite the dataset formally to give credit to data producers and increase transparency. 发表论文时正式引用数据集，体现对数据生产者的学术认可，提升研究透明度。
  • Follow the data citation standard: Author (Year). Title. Repository. DOI. 遵循数据引用标准格式：作者 (年份). 标题. 数据仓库. DOI。
Deep Dive: Repository Curation & Peer Review 深度拓展：仓库策展与同行评审

Curatorial review: Professional repositories conduct formal curatorial review, including metadata completeness, de-identification quality, file format sustainability and ethical compliance. 策展审核： 专业仓库会进行正式的策展审核，包括元数据完整性、去标识化质量、 文件格式可持续性、伦理合规性等维度。

Data peer review: Some high-standard repositories implement data peer review, where domain experts evaluate the scientific quality and reusability of the dataset. 数据同行评审： 部分高标准仓库实行数据同行评审，由领域专家评估数据集的科学质量与可复用性。

Hazel’s Question Hazel 的疑问

If data is in a repository, shouldn’t everyone be able to download it freely? Why do we need “controlled access” with applications and reviews? 数据放进仓库不就应该人人都能自由下载吗？ 为什么还要「受控访问」，还要申请审核？

Principle: Openness is a goal, not an absolute. For sensitive qualitative data, controlled access balances public benefit with participant protection. 原则：开放是目标，而非绝对。敏感定性数据通过受控访问，平衡公共利益与受访者保护。

4.5 Controlled Access Ethics & Governance 4.5 受控访问伦理与治理

Three Access Modes 三级访问模式

Open Access 开放访问

Anyone can download the data freely without registration. 任何人无需注册即可自由下载数据。

Applicable to: Fully de-identified, non-sensitive data with no risk of re-identification 适用：完全脱敏、无敏感内容、无重识别风险的数据

Registered Access 注册访问

Users must register with real identity and agree to terms of use before accessing data. 用户需实名注册并同意使用条款，方可访问数据。

Applicable to: Moderately sensitive data with low re-identification risk 适用：中等敏感、重识别风险较低的数据

Controlled Access 受控访问

Users must submit a formal application stating research purpose; access is granted only after review and approval. 用户需提交正式申请说明研究用途，审核通过后方可访问数据。

Applicable to: Highly sensitive qualitative data with potential re-identification risk 适用：高度敏感、存在潜在重识别风险的定性数据

Controlled Access Review Process 受控访问审核流程

1

Formal application submission 正式申请提交

Applicant provides research project description, methodology, intended use and institutional affiliation. 申请者提供研究项目说明、方法、预期用途与所属机构。

2

Ethical & scientific review 伦理与科学审核

Data access committee reviews whether the purpose is legitimate and whether risks to participants are minimized. 数据访问委员会审核用途是否正当、对受访者的风险是否最小化。

3

Data Use Agreement signing 签署数据使用协议

Approved users sign a legally binding DUA specifying usage constraints and confidentiality obligations. 通过审核的用户签署具有约束力的使用协议，明确使用限制与保密义务。

4

Secure data delivery & monitoring 安全交付与使用监控

Data is delivered through a secure channel; access logs are maintained for compliance tracking. 通过安全渠道交付数据，保留访问日志用于合规追踪。

Core Clauses of a Data Use Agreement (DUA) 数据使用协议核心条款

  • Purpose limitation: Data may only be used for the stated research project. 用途限定：仅可用于约定的研究项目。
  • No re-identification: Explicit prohibition on attempting to identify individual participants. 禁止重识别：明确禁止尝试识别受访者个人身份。
  • No secondary transfer: Data may not be shared or transferred to third parties. 禁止二次传播：不得将数据共享或转发给第三方。
  • Security obligations: Maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures. 安全义务：保持适当的技术与组织安全措施。
  • Citation requirement: Formal citation of the dataset in all research outputs. 引用要求：所有研究成果中正式引用该数据集。
  • Destruction upon completion: Securely destroy all copies of the data when the project ends. 到期销毁：项目结束后安全销毁所有数据副本。
Deep Dive: Governance Models & Data Justice 深度拓展：治理模式与数据正义

Data Access Committees: Professional repositories typically establish independent DACs (Data Access Committees) with mixed membership including researchers, ethicists and community representatives. 数据访问委员会： 专业仓库通常设立独立的 DAC（数据访问委员会）， 成员包括研究者、伦理学家与社群代表。

Data justice perspective: Controlled access is not just about “restricting data”, but about upholding the autonomy and dignity of research participants, especially marginalized and vulnerable groups. 数据正义视角： 受控访问不只是「限制数据」，更是维护研究参与者、 尤其是边缘弱势群体的自主权与尊严。
