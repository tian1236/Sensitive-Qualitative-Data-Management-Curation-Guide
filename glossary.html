Glossary术语表

Terms are simplified for navigation; legal meaning depends on the governing instrument.下列解释用于导航；法律含义以适用法律文本为准。

Anonymisation / 匿名化
Processing that renders a person no longer identifiable under the applicable legal test. It is stronger than merely removing names.依适用法律判断，使个人不再可被识别的处理；强度高于单纯删除姓名。
Pseudonymisation / 假名化
Replacing identifiers while keeping separate additional information that can restore the link. Pseudonymised data generally remains personal data.替换标识符，同时分离保存能够恢复关联的附加信息；假名化资料通常仍属于个人资料。
De-identification / 去标识化
A broad operational term for reducing identification risk; it does not by itself prove legal anonymisation.降低识别风险的广义操作术语；本身并不能证明已达到法律上的匿名化。
Residual risk / 剩余风险
Disclosure or harm risk remaining after controls have been applied.实施控制措施后仍然存在的泄露或伤害风险。
Controlled access / 受控访问
Access granted only after eligibility, purpose and safeguards are reviewed, usually with enforceable terms and monitoring.经资格、目的与保障审查后才授权的访问，通常配有可执行条款和监督。
DPIA / 数据保护影响评估
A structured assessment of high-risk personal-data processing, required under GDPR in specified circumstances.对高风险个人数据处理开展的结构化评估；GDPR在特定情形下要求进行。
DUA / 数据使用协议
An agreement defining permitted use, security, confidentiality, publication, incident and termination obligations.规定允许用途、安全、保密、发表、事件及终止义务的协议。
