Hazel’s Dilemma Hazel 的困境

Hazel is a master’s student in Social Sciences at Woodland University, conducting narrative interviews with forest dwellers about habitat and life experiences. She originally thought removing names was enough to protect privacy — until her advisor warned that habitat locations, unique life events and community ties can all re-identify individuals. She needs a complete, actionable roadmap from compliance to archiving. Hazel 是森林大学社科系硕士生，正在做森林居民栖息地与生活叙事访谈研究。 她原本以为删掉受访者名字就足够安全，直到导师提醒： 栖息地位置、独特经历、社群关系都可能反向定位到个体。 从合规管理到公开归档，她需要一套完整可落地的操作方案。

Beginner Thesis Path 新手毕业路径

For graduate students and early-career researchers fulfilling basic thesis and research compliance requirements. 适合人文社科研究生、青年研究者，满足毕业论文与常规研究的基础合规需求。
