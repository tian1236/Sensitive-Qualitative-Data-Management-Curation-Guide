Module 2: Full Lifecycle Data Management 模块二：数据全生命周期管理

A structured operational guide across five stages — collection, processing, storage, sharing, and retention, archiving or destruction. Use it to document controls across the qualitative research data lifecycle. 以五个阶段组织操作指引：收集、处理、存储、共享，以及保留、归档或销毁， 用于记录定性研究数据全生命周期的控制措施。

Hazel’s Mistake Hazel 踩的坑

Hazel's consent form offered two options: “I agree” or “I do not agree.” She thought that covered everything. It didn't. Hazel 的知情同意书只有两个选项：“同意”或“不同意”。她以为这覆盖了一切。其实没有。

Core lesson: Real consent is not one-size-fits-all. Breaking authorisation into independent tiers respects participants' different comfort levels — and makes it easier to ask for re-consent when new uses come up later. 核心要点：真正的同意不是一刀切的。把授权拆分为独立层级，既尊重参与者不同的接受程度——也为后续新用途出现时的再同意铺平道路。

Informed Consent: Ongoing Negotiation & Tiered Authorisation知情同意：持续协商与分层授权

In qualitative research, consent is not a form signed once. It is an ongoing negotiation across recruitment, fieldwork or interviews, and later uses of data.定性研究的知情同意不是一张可以一次性签署的表格。它更适合作为一个贯穿招募、田野/访谈和后续使用阶段的持续协商过程。

Before starting, explain the purpose, participation, risks, privacy arrangements and right to withdraw. Keep information understandable and communication two-way; do not reduce consent to one-way disclosure.研究者在开始前应说明研究目的、参与内容、风险、隐私处理与退出权，并确保参与者的决定是自愿的。信息应当易于理解且保持双向沟通，避免把同意简化为单向的信息披露。

Tiered Authorisation Structure分层授权结构

Replace a single yes/no option with independent permissions, so participants can choose the scope they accept.不只用单一的“同意/不同意”选项，而是把授权拆分为独立层级，让参与者可以精确选择自己愿意接受的范围。

01

Participation基础参与层

Agree to take part in the interview, observation or focus group itself.同意参与访谈、观察或焦点小组本身。

02

Recording & Transcription记录与转录层

Agree to recording, automated transcription or AI-assisted processing; explain platform security for digital collection.同意录音/录像、自动转录或 AI 辅助处理；数字化收集需说明平台安全。

03

Quotation & Publication引用与发表层

Allow direct quotations, or request paraphrase or review before use.允许直接引用，或要求使用前改写或复核。

04

Sharing & Archiving共享与归档层

Agree to de-identified sharing, repository deposit or controlled access.同意去标识化后的数据共享、存入数据仓库或受控访问。

05

Future Reuse未来再使用层

For research beyond the current purpose, re-consent is often better than blanket authorisation.用于当前项目之外的未来研究时，再同意通常优于一次性笼统授权。

Initial Consent & Re-consent初始同意与再同意

For open-ended or long-term research, begin with general consent for core activities. When direction changes, new uses emerge or different secondary use is proposed, seek re-consent at that point rather than asking participants to accept every unknown use at the start.对于开放式或长期研究，建议先取得覆盖基本研究活动的一般同意。当研究重点调整、新用途出现或拟开展不同形式的二次使用时，应在该时间点通过再同意补充授权——而不是在开始时要求参与者同意所有未知事项。

When to Raise the Bar何时提高同意标准

  • Cross-national research:跨国研究： align legal frameworks, ethics norms and disciplinary traditions across jurisdictions.对齐不同法域的法律框架、伦理规范和学科传统。
  • Vulnerable or access-limited populations:弱势或可及性受限群体： use understandable materials and person-centred communication; involve supporters when appropriate.使用可理解的材料，必要时邀请支持者参与，优先以人为中心的沟通。
  • Remote and digital consent:远程与电子同意： address platform security, legal validity, usability and researcher training for e-consent, video or AI transcription.使用电子同意、视频会议或 AI 转录时，额外处理平台安全、法律效力、可用性与研究者培训问题。

Tiered Informed Consent Design 分层知情同意设计

Instead of a single blanket consent, split authorization into independent tiers to respect participant autonomy while preserving future data use options. 摒弃单一笼统的同意格式，将授权拆分为独立层级，既尊重受访者的自主选择权， 也为后续的数据使用保留空间。

Level 1 — Project Use Only 一级：仅本项目使用

Data may only be used for the stated research project and thesis. 数据仅用于约定的研究项目与学位论文。

Level 2 — Team Collaborative Use 二级：团队合作使用

Data may be shared with approved collaborators within the research team. 数据可共享给研究团队内已授权的合作人员。

Level 3 — De-identified Archiving 三级：去标识化归档

De-identified data may be deposited in a controlled-access repository for secondary research. 去标识化后的数据可存入受控访问数据仓库，用于二次研究。

Level 4 — Quotation Permission 四级：引用授权

Anonymized excerpts may be quoted directly in publications. 允许在出版物中直接引用去标识化后的原文片段。

Collection Protocols & Standards 收集规范与标准

  • File naming convention: Use format ProjectID_DataType_ParticipantID_Version; never use real names in filenames. 统一使用 项目编号_数据类型_受访者编号_版本号 格式，禁止文件名出现真实姓名。
  • Minimum necessary principle: Only collect information directly relevant to the research question; avoid unnecessary private details. 遵循最小必要原则，只收集与研究问题直接相关的信息，不追问无关隐私。
  • Field note standard: Clearly separate objective observations from researcher inferences and subjective judgments. 田野笔记严格区分「客观观察事实」与「研究者主观推断」。

Sources: 来源：PCPD cross-border guidance · GDPR Chapter V

Common Pitfalls 常见避坑指南

  • ⚠️ Vague consent language that limits future data sharing and archiving options ⚠️ 知情同意表述笼统，限制后续数据共享与归档空间
  • ⚠️ Default recording filenames with timestamps and GPS locations that leak identity ⚠️ 录音设备默认文件名带时间、地点信息，间接泄露身份
  • ⚠️ Collecting excessive personal information unrelated to the research question ⚠️ 收集与研究主题无关的过多个人隐私信息

Deep Dive: Advanced Ethical & Legal Details 深度拓展：进阶伦理与法律细节

Verbal consent validity: Under PDPO, verbal consent is acceptable in certain contexts, but written documentation is strongly recommended for sensitive research to establish clear evidence of agreement. 口头同意的有效性： 在PDPO框架下，部分场景可采用口头同意，但敏感研究强烈建议书面记录， 作为授权的明确证据。

Re-consent in longitudinal research: For long-term follow-up studies, re-consent should be obtained at each major phase, especially if data use plans change. 纵向研究的二次同意： 长期追踪研究应在每个重要阶段重新获取同意，尤其是数据使用计划发生变化时。

Relational ethics in vulnerable communities: Beyond formal consent, researchers must consider trust dynamics and avoid exploiting power imbalances in field research with marginalized groups. 弱势社群的关系伦理： 除了正式同意，研究者还需考虑信任关系，避免在边缘群体田野研究中利用权力不对等。

4-Step Basic De-identification 基础去标识化四步法

1

Remove direct identifiers 删除直接标识符

Names, contact information, exact addresses, ID numbers, social media handles. 姓名、联系方式、具体地址、证件号、社交账号。

2

Apply consistent pseudonyms 统一伪名替换

Use fixed IDs or pseudonyms for all individuals and locations; maintain a separate encrypted key file. 所有人物、地点使用固定代号/伪名；单独保存密钥表并加密。

3

Generalize contextual details 上下文模糊化

Specific locations → regions; exact dates → time periods; specific institutions → sector categories. 具体地点→区域；具体日期→时间段；具体机构→行业类别。

4

Remove unique identifying traits 移除独特特征

Remove rare occupations, unique life events and other details that could enable re-identification. 删除罕见职业、独特人生经历等可反向定位个人的信息。

Transcription & Version Control 转录与版本管理

  • Unified transcription rules: Define consistent standards for dialect, pauses, non-verbal sounds and annotations. 制定统一的转录规范，对方言、停顿、非语言声音、标注方式做明确约定。
  • Dual version separation: Maintain raw unprocessed data and de-identified data in separate folders with clear version labels. 原始版与去标识化版分文件夹存放，标注清晰的版本号。
  • Secure processing environment: Never process raw sensitive data on public computers, shared devices or unencrypted cloud services. 禁止在公共电脑、共享设备、未加密云盘上处理原始敏感数据。

Processing Stage Checklist 处理阶段自查清单

Deep Dive: Advanced Processing Methods 深度拓展：进阶处理方法

Transcription staff management: External transcriptionists must sign confidentiality agreements and should only receive de-identified materials when possible. 转录人员管理： 外部转录人员必须签署保密协议，尽可能只提供去标识化后的材料。

Automated de-identification tools: NLP tools can assist with removing named entities, but manual review is mandatory for qualitative data due to contextual re-identification risks. 自动去标识化工具： NLP工具可辅助移除命名实体，但定性数据因存在语境重识别风险，人工复核必不可少。

Voice data risks: Voiceprints are unique identifiers. Audio data intended for sharing requires voice alteration, though this does not guarantee full de-identification. 语音数据的特殊风险： 声纹属于独特标识符。用于共享的音频数据需做变声处理，但无法保证完全去标识化。

Practical Security Rules 实操安全规范

  • Full-disk encryption: Enable system-level encryption (BitLocker / FileVault) on all devices storing sensitive data. 开启系统级磁盘加密（BitLocker / FileVault）。
  • File-level encryption: Individually encrypt highly sensitive files with strong passwords; store keys separately. 高敏感文件单独加密，密钥分开存放。
  • Principle of least privilege: Grant access only to those who need it; research assistants should only work with de-identified data. 最小权限原则，学生助理仅可接触去标识化数据。
  • Institutional cloud first: Prioritize university-authorized cloud services; avoid consumer-grade public cloud drives for sensitive data. 优先使用学校授权云盘，慎用消费级公共云盘存敏感数据。

Storage Stage Checklist 存储阶段自查清单

Deep Dive: Professional Storage Solutions 深度拓展：专业存储方案

End-to-end encryption tools: For high-risk data, use zero-knowledge encryption tools to ensure even service providers cannot access content. 端到端加密工具： 高风险数据推荐使用零知识加密工具，确保服务商也无法读取内容。

Format migration for long-term preservation: Proprietary file formats may become unreadable over time. Plan periodic format migration to open, sustainable formats for long-term retention. 长期存储的格式迁移： 专有文件格式可能随软件淘汰而无法读取，长期留存需定期迁移至开放可持续格式。

Physical media security: Offline hard drives and paper records should be stored in locked, fire-resistant cabinets with controlled access and inventory logs. 物理介质安全： 离线硬盘、纸质资料应存放于上锁的防火柜中，控制访问权限并保留出入记录。

Three Sharing Scenarios & Requirements 三类共享场景与对应要求

Scenario 1 场景一

Intra-team / Internal Sharing 团队内 / 校内共享

  • Implement role-based access control 实行基于角色的权限控制
  • All personnel sign confidentiality commitments 所有人员签署保密承诺
  • Share only the minimum necessary data version 仅共享必要级别的数据版本
  • Prohibit forwarding or secondary distribution without authorization 禁止擅自转发与二次传播
Scenario 2 场景二

Cross-institutional Collaborative Sharing 跨机构合作共享

  • Share de-identified data only; never send raw identifiable data 仅共享去标识化版本，绝不发送原始可识别数据
  • Sign a formal Data Use Agreement (DUA) before sharing 共享前签署正式的数据使用协议（DUA）
  • Clearly define purpose, scope and duration of use 明确约定使用目的、范围与期限
  • Verify the recipient’s data security capabilities 核实接收方的数据安全保障条件
Scenario 3 场景三

Public Archiving & Open Sharing 公开归档与开放共享

  • Must have explicit informed consent authorization from participants 必须获得受访者明确的知情同意授权
  • Complete corresponding tier of de-identification 完成对应级别的去标识化处理
  • Prepare complete standardized metadata documentation 准备完整的标准化元数据文档
  • Select appropriate access mode (open / registered / application-based) 选择合适的访问模式（公开/注册/申请制）

Cross-Border Data Transfer Considerations 跨境数据共享注意事项

  • Hong Kong's current Data Protection Principles remain relevant to an overseas transfer. PDPO section 33 is not yet in force; document recipient due diligence and contractual safeguards in line with PCPD guidance. 香港现行资料保障原则在跨境转移中仍然相关；PDPO第33条尚未生效，应依私隐专员指引记录接收方尽职调查及合同保障。
  • Where GDPR applies and Chapter V is engaged, use an appropriate mechanism such as an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses or another valid route, with supplementary measures where required. 在GDPR适用且触发第五章时，应采用充分性决定、标准合同条款或其他有效机制，并在需要时落实补充措施。
  • As a general principle, avoid transferring raw sensitive data across borders. Transfer only de-identified versions when necessary. 通用原则：敏感原始数据尽量不跨境传输，确需传输优先使用去标识化版本。

Resource: 来源：PDPO / PCPD · GDPR Arts. 44–45

Common Pitfalls 常见避坑指南

  • ⚠️ Sharing raw data directly for convenience, ignoring de-identification requirements ⚠️ 图方便直接共享原始数据，忽略脱敏要求
  • ⚠️ Sharing without a written agreement, leading to uncontrolled secondary dissemination ⚠️ 不签协议就共享，导致二次传播失控
  • ⚠️ Sharing beyond the scope of original informed consent ⚠️ 共享范围超出原始知情同意的授权边界
  • ⚠️ Transferring sensitive raw data across borders without compliance review ⚠️ 未经合规审查就跨境传输原始敏感数据

5 2.5 Data Destruction 2.5 数据销毁

Determining Destruction Timing 销毁时机判断

  • Legal requirement: PDPO mandates that personal data shall not be kept longer than is necessary for the purpose it was collected. PDPO 规定个人数据留存不得超过收集目的所需的期限。
  • Ethics requirement: No universal period applies. Follow the approved protocol, law, institutional and funder rules; record the trigger date and review date. 不存在通用年限。应遵循获批方案、法律、机构及资助方规则，并记录起算日与复核日。
  • Extensions: Retention may be extended for pending litigation, audit requirements or ongoing research. 未结诉讼、审计要求、持续研究等情形可延长留存。

Resource: 来源：PDPO / PCPD · ICO. Principle (e) · DRP / CMA

Destruction Record Keeping 销毁记录留存

Maintain a formal destruction record as evidence of compliance, including the following information: 保留正式的销毁记录作为合规凭证，应包含以下信息：

  • Date and time of destruction 销毁执行日期与时间
  • Description and volume of data destroyed 销毁数据的内容与数量
  • Destruction method used 采用的销毁方式
  • Person executing and supervising the destruction 执行人和监督人
  • Confirmation that all backups have been destroyed 所有备份均已销毁的确认

Destruction Stage Checklist 销毁阶段自查清单

Deep Dive: Boundaries & Exceptions 深度拓展：边界与例外情形

Exceptions to destruction: Data may be retained beyond the normal period for legal holds, judicial requests, public interest exemptions or archival preservation with ethical approval. 销毁的例外情形： 司法冻结、法律调取、公共利益豁免、经伦理批准的档案留存等，可超期留存。

Digital residue problem: Cloud backups, cached copies, chat history and temporary files are often overlooked. Conduct a full sweep across all storage locations before finalizing destruction. 数字残留问题： 云备份、缓存副本、聊天记录、临时文件常被遗漏，销毁前需全面排查所有存储位置。
