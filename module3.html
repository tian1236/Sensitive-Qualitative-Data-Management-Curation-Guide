Hazel’s Question Hazel 的疑问

My university is based in the woodland region, and all my participants are local residents. Which rules do I actually need to follow? What if I collaborate with researchers overseas? 我的学校在森林地区，受访者也都是本地居民。我到底要遵守哪些规则？ 如果和境外研究者合作，又会有什么不同？

Principle: determine the scope of each law separately. Establishment, processing activity, participant location, targeting or monitoring, funding and data transfers may all matter. 原则：分别判断每部法律的适用范围。机构设立地、处理活动、受访者所在地、是否定向提供服务或监测、资助条件与数据跨境都可能产生影响。

Jurisdiction Self-Assessment 管辖权两步判定

1

Institutional location 机构所在地

Establishment and the location of processing are important connecting factors, but do not decide every case alone. Institutional ethics and sponsor requirements must also be checked. 机构设立地和数据处理地是重要连接因素，但不能单独决定所有情形；还需核查机构伦理与资助方要求。

2

Participant location 受访者所在地

Participant location may create additional obligations, but location alone does not automatically make every foreign law applicable. Check the law's territorial-scope test and the actual processing activity. 受访者所在地可能产生额外义务，但并非只要受访者在境外就自动适用当地全部法律；应结合该法律的属地范围条款与实际处理活动判断。

Deep Dive: Legislative Logic Differences 深度拓展：立法逻辑差异

Common law vs. civil law traditions: PDPO follows a common law approach with principle-based regulation and flexible interpretation. GDPR is rooted in civil law, with detailed prescriptive rules and strong enforcement. 普通法与大陆法传统： PDPO 遵循普通法路径，以原则性监管为主，解释灵活； GDPR 根植于大陆法体系，规则详尽、执行力度强。

Legal compliance vs. ethical duty: Data protection laws set the minimum legal baseline, while research ethics impose higher standards of care, dignity and justice for participants. Ethics goes beyond what is legally required. 法律合规与伦理义务： 数据保护法设定最低法律底线，研究伦理则对受试者的尊严、公正与福祉提出更高要求。 伦理要求高于法律底线。

Global convergence trend: Worldwide data protection regimes are converging toward stronger individual rights, stricter sensitive data rules and greater cross-border cooperation. 全球趋同趋势： 全球数据保护体系正逐步趋同，整体朝向更强的个人权利、更严的敏感数据规则、 更多的跨境合作方向发展。

3.2 Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO)

Hong Kong's principal legislation governing the collection, holding, processing and use of personal data.香港规管个人资料收集、持有、处理与使用的主要法律。

Core Scope of Application 核心适用范围

  • Applies where the statutory connection to Hong Kong is met. Confirm the role of the data user, where the processing occurs and any applicable exemptions. 在满足香港法定连接因素时适用；应核实资料使用者角色、处理地点及适用豁免。
  • Interview recordings, transcripts and field notes containing identifiable information all qualify as personal data. 包含可识别信息的访谈录音、转录稿、田野笔记，均属于个人资料。
  • Applies to both electronic and physical forms of personal data. 电子与实体形式的个人资料均受规管。

Six Data Protection Principles 六项数据保护原则

Research processing that falls within the PDPO must comply with the six Data Protection Principles. 属于《个人资料（私隐）条例》规管范围的研究处理活动，须遵守六项资料保障原则。

1. Fair and Lawful Collection 1. 公平合法地收集

Data must be collected by fair and lawful means, with transparency about purpose and use. No deception or undue influence on participants. 必须以公平合法的方式收集数据，对收集目的与用途保持透明，不得欺骗或不当影响受访者。

2. Accuracy and Retention 2. 准确性与保留期限

Take practicable steps to keep data accurate and do not retain personal data longer than necessary. 采取切实可行步骤确保资料准确，并不得把个人资料保留超过实际需要的时间。

3. Use of Personal Data 3. 个人资料的使用

Use data only for the purpose stated on collection or a directly related purpose, unless prescribed consent or another legal permission applies. 个人资料只可用于收集时说明的目的或与其直接有关的目的，除非取得订明同意或另有法律许可。

4. Data Security 4. 资料保安

Take practicable steps against unauthorised or accidental access, processing, erasure, loss or use. 采取切实可行步骤，防止未经授权或意外地查阅、处理、删除、丧失或使用个人资料。

5. Openness and Transparency 5. 公开与透明

Maintain generally available information about personal-data policies, practices, purposes and data categories held. 公开个人资料政策与实务、使用目的，以及所持个人资料的种类。

6. Access and Correction 6. 查阅与改正

Give data subjects rights of access to and correction of their personal data, subject to statutory procedures and exemptions. 按法定程序与豁免，保障资料当事人查阅及改正个人资料的权利。

Higher-Risk Personal Data & Cross-Border Guidance 较高风险个人资料与跨境指引

Risk-proportionate safeguards 与风险相称的保障

PDPO does not use GDPR's special-category structure. Apply stronger security, access and transparency controls where disclosure could cause greater harm. PDPO没有采用GDPR的特殊类别结构；若泄露可能造成较大伤害，应加强保安、权限和透明度控制。

Cross-border transfer rules 跨境传输规则

Section 33's transfer restrictions are not yet in force. Existing DPP obligations remain relevant, and PCPD recommends due diligence, contractual safeguards and comparable protection as good practice. 第33条的跨境限制尚未生效；现行资料保障原则仍然相关，私隐专员建议以尽职调查、合同保障和相若保护作为良好实践。

Deep Dive: Research Exemption & Enforcement 深度拓展：研究豁免与执法处罚

Research exemption conditions: Purely academic research with no direct decisions affecting individuals may be exempt from certain access and correction requirements, provided confidentiality is maintained and results are published only in aggregated form. 研究豁免条件： 纯学术研究、不会对个人产生直接决策影响的，在保密且仅以汇总形式发布结果的前提下， 可豁免部分查阅与更正要求。

Enforcement and penalties: Violations may result in administrative fines, enforcement notices, and in serious cases criminal liability for intentional unlawful disclosure. 执法与处罚： 违规可能导致行政罚款、执行通知，严重情况下故意非法泄露还可能承担刑事责任。

3.3 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

The European Union's rights-based data protection regulation. Its territorial scope is determined by Article 3, not simply by a participant's nationality or location.欧盟以权利为核心的数据保护法规；其属地范围由第3条判断，不能只看受访者国籍或所在地。

Scope of Application 适用范围

GDPR applies to processing in the context of an EU/EEA establishment and, in specified circumstances, to organisations outside the EU/EEA that offer goods or services to, or monitor the behaviour of, people in the Union. Participant location alone is not a complete test. GDPR适用于欧盟/欧洲经济区机构业务活动中的处理，并在特定情况下适用于向欧盟境内个人提供商品或服务、或监测其行为的境外机构。受访者所在地本身不是完整判断标准。

Article 9 — Special Category Personal Data 第 9 条 — 特殊类别个人数据

Processing of special category data is prohibited in principle, unless one of the explicit derogations applies. 特殊类别数据的处理原则上被禁止，除非符合明确的豁免情形。

Key point for qualitative research: Article 9 covers only the listed special categories. Trauma narratives or alleged illegal activity are not automatically Article 9 data unless they reveal a listed category; criminal-offence data is addressed separately by Article 10. 定性研究要点：第9条只涵盖法定列举的特殊类别。创伤叙事或涉嫌违法行为不会自动成为第9条数据，除非其揭示了列举类别；刑事定罪与违法相关数据另由第10条规管。

List of Article 9 Special Category Data 第9条特殊类别数据清单

  • Racial / ethnic origin种族/民族出身
  • Political opinions, beliefs政治观点、信仰
  • Religious / philosophical beliefs宗教/哲学信仰
  • Trade union membership工会会员身份
  • Genetic & biometric identification data基因、生物识别数据
  • Health, medical, psychological records健康、医疗、心理记录
  • Sex life & sexual orientation性生活与性取向

Valid Legal Bases for Qualitative Research 定性研究适用的合法处理依据

1. Explicit Consent (Art.6(1)a + Art.9(2)a)

When consent is used, it must be demonstrably freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous; Article 9 consent must also be explicit. Withdrawal must be possible and as easy as giving consent. A signed form is one way to document consent, not a universal GDPR requirement. 采用同意作为依据时，必须能够证明其为自愿、具体、知情且明确；第9条所称同意还须达到“明示”标准。撤回同意应当可行，且不得比给予同意更困难。签字表格是记录方式之一，并非 GDPR 的普遍强制要求。

2. Research basis authorised by law (e.g. Art.6(1)e + Art.9(2)j)

Available only where relevant Union or Member State law authorises it and its conditions are met. Article 89 safeguards include data minimisation and, where feasible, pseudonymisation. 只有相关欧盟或成员国法律授权且满足其条件时才能使用；第89条保障包括数据最小化，以及在可行时采用假名化。

Cross-Border Transfer & Penalties 跨境传输与处罚规则

Cross-border rules跨境传输要求

Chapter V permits transfers through adequacy decisions, appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or binding corporate rules, and limited derogations. Assess the correct mechanism and any supplementary measures. 第五章允许通过充分性决定、标准合同条款或约束性公司规则等适当保障，以及有限的例外进行传输；应评估正确机制及所需补充措施。

Maximum Penalty最高处罚标准

For infringements listed in Article 83(5), the upper tier is €20 million or 4% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher. Actual enforcement depends on the facts and Article 83 criteria. 对第83(5)条列明的违规，上限为2000万欧元或全球年营业额4%，取较高者；实际执法取决于事实及第83条衡量因素。

Deep Dive: Art.89 Scientific Research Safeguards深度拓展：第89条科研专项保护措施

Article 89 requires appropriate safeguards for public-interest archiving, scientific or historical research and statistics. Union or Member State law may provide specified derogations subject to conditions. Relevant safeguards may include: 第89条要求公共利益归档、科学或历史研究及统计处理采用适当保障；欧盟或成员国法律可在特定条件下规定有限克减。相关保障可包括：

  • Pseudonymisation where the research purpose can be fulfilled in that manner在研究目的能够实现时采用假名化
  • Strict access control with role-based permissions基于角色的严格访问权限管控
  • Data minimisation: no collection of unnecessary identifying details数据最小化，不采集多余识别信息
  • Retention limited to the minimum period required for research and archival留存期限压缩至研究与归档所需最短时长
  • Procedural limits on combining research datasets with external personal records限制研究数据集与外部个人档案合并使用

Data-subject rights and any research derogations must be assessed under the relevant processing basis and applicable Union or Member State law; they should not be presented as universally absolute or universally waived. 数据主体权利及研究克减应结合处理依据与适用的欧盟或成员国法律判断，不能表述为一律绝对或一律豁免。

3.4 Institutional Review Board (IRB) Human Research Ethics Framework3.4 机构伦理审查委员会（IRB）人类研究伦理框架

Ethics oversight complements data-protection law. Before collection, obtain the required determination—such as not-human-subjects, exempt, expedited or convened review—under the rules that govern the institution and project.伦理监督是数据保护法的补充。采集前应依机构与项目适用规则取得相应认定，例如不属于人类受试者研究、豁免、快速审查或全体会议审查。

Four Core Ethical Principles 四大核心伦理原则

1. Respect for Persons

Autonomy of participants; voluntary informed consent; special additional protections for vulnerable populations (minors, prisoners, undocumented persons, trauma survivors). No coercion, manipulation or hidden study objectives. 尊重受试者自主决定权；自愿知情同意；弱势群体（未成年人、囚犯、无证件人士、创伤幸存者）额外保护。禁止胁迫、诱导、隐瞒研究目的。

2. Beneficence

Maximise research social value and minimise physical, psychological and social risks. Assess whether appropriate support resources or referral information should be included for the actual risk profile. 最大化研究社会价值并最小化身心与社会风险；根据实际风险判断是否应提供适当支持资源或转介信息。

3. Justice

Fair recruitment and participant selection; avoid over-reliance on marginalised groups to bear all research risks while privileged groups receive research benefits. Equitable access to research compensation and support services. 公平招募受试者，避免仅由边缘群体承担全部研究风险、优势群体独享研究收益；补偿与支持资源公平分配。

4. Integrity & Confidentiality

Full transparency in research design, data handling and publication; strict confidentiality measures to prevent identification of participants in transcripts, field notes and published outputs. 研究设计、数据处理、成果发表全程透明；严格保密措施，防止转录稿、田野笔记、出版物识别出受试者身份。

Obtain legally effective informed consent unless the governing rules and ethics committee permit an exemption, waiver or alteration. Documentation is often written, but an IRB may waive signed documentation in specified circumstances. Data Confidentiality & De-identification Ethics Rules 数据保密与脱敏伦理规范

  • Remove, replace or securely separate direct identifiers according to the approved protocol and documented risk assessment; retain only when justified and protected. 依照获批方案与书面风险评估删除、替换或安全分离直接标识符；只有在有充分理由并采取保护时才保留。
  • Assess contextual identifiers—such as unique roles, rare conditions and specific locations—and remove, generalise, separate or restrict access where needed to manage disclosure risk. 评估独特职业、罕见病症、特定地点等语境标识符，并在需要时删除、泛化、分离或限制访问，以控制泄露风险。
  • Encrypt raw recordings and apply the retention or destruction schedule approved for the project; transcription completion does not create a universal deletion deadline. 加密原始录音，并执行项目获批的留存或销毁计划；转录完成并不会产生一律适用的删除期限。
  • Store the re-identification key separately with least-privilege access assigned to named authorised roles, not automatically to one universal job title. 将重新识别密钥分离存储，并按最小权限授予明确的获授权角色，而不是一律限定为某个通用职位。

  • Suspension of affected research activities while the matter is assessed在调查期间暂停受影响的研究活动

  • Publication correction or retraction where journal standards are breached在违反期刊规范时更正或撤回论文
  • Institutional discipline proportionate to the findings与调查结论相称的机构处分
  • Legal liability where the applicable elements are established在符合相应法律构成要件时承担法律责任

IRB violations also create compound legal risk alongside PDPO / GDPR data protection regulatory penalties. 违反IRB伦理要求将叠加PDPO、GDPR等数据保护法规的法律处罚风险。
